# Privacy Policy — Matey Tanks

Last updated: 2026-11-19

**Matey Tanks** (`com.pockettanksduel.game`, published by VaelNTN Studios) is a free, ad-free, 2D artillery tank game with destructible terrain, dynamic wind, 5-tank battles, and optional online leaderboards.

## Data We Collect
- **No personal information** (no name, email, phone number, contacts, or location) is collected or sold.
- **No third-party advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or in-app purchases** are included.
- **Local storage:** Your chosen commander nickname, tank skin, sound setting, and local match records are saved solely on your device.
- **Optional online leaderboard:** When submitting a high score to the optional global leaderboard, only a random device identifier, your chosen commander nickname, score, win count, streak, and tank skin ID are sent over HTTPS to the VaelNTN leaderboard worker.

## Children & Privacy
Matey Tanks does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults. You can clear all local data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings.

## Contact
For questions or score removal requests, please open an issue at https://github.com/VaelNTN/pocket-tanks-duel.
