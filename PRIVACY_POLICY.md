## English (Google Play Store Review)

### 1. Overview
Pocket Tanks Duel is a free 2D destructible terrain artillery tank duel with **no real-money purchases**, **no advertisements**, and **no third-party analytics SDKs**.

### 2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
Your game progress, personal best scores, display name, and optional custom server URL are stored locally on your device using WebView `localStorage`.

### 3. Online Highscore Leaderboard (Optional)
If online highscores are enabled (via the developer's Cloudflare Worker API), the app transmits only:
- A randomly generated anonymous `deviceId` (created locally on first launch)
- Your chosen display name / nickname
- Your numeric game score

This data is used strictly to display the online leaderboard inside the app. We do **not** collect your real name, email address, location, contacts, or advertising ID.

### 4. Data Sharing & Selling
We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

### 5. Data Deletion
- **Local data:** Clear app storage or uninstall the app.
- **Leaderboard entry:** Open an issue on `https://github.com/VaelNTN/pocket-tanks-duel/issues` mentioning your display name to request removal.

## Nederlands

### 1. Overzicht
Pocket Tanks Duel is een gratis arcade-spel zonder advertenties, zonder trackers van derden en zonder aankopen met echt geld.

### 2. Lokale opslag
Spelvoortgang en spelersnaam worden lokaal op je toestel opgeslagen (`localStorage`).

### 3. Online Highscores
Bij het ophalen/versturen van online highscores verstuurt de app alleen een willekeurig aangemaakt lokaal `deviceId`, je gekozen spelersnaam en je score naar de eigen Cloudflare-highscore-API. Er worden geen persoonsgegevens of advertentie-ID's verzameld of gedeeld.
